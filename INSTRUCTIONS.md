2. Randomly draw samples (with replacement) of size n = 5, n = 40, and n = 100, 10 times each.
3. For each sample size:
   1. Obtain the sample means.
   2. Create a histogram with density curve of the sample means. Interpret.
   3. Calculate the mean and standard error of the sample means. Interpret.
4. Discuss how the shape and characteristics of the sampling distribution change with different sample sizes.
